About the School

Shaping Future Leaders
Since 1999

Air Force Comprehensive School (AFCS), Uyo is a premier co-educational boarding secondary school established in January 1999 under the Nigerian Air Force Directorate of Education. Located in Uyo, the capital of Akwa Ibom State, the school provides comprehensive secondary education with military discipline and academic excellence.


The school is staffed by a combination of Nigerian Air Force military instructors and civilian teachers, ensuring both academic rigour and structured discipline that prepares students for higher education and national service.
